# Third-party notices

## Media assets

Tabataki currently contains no separately downloaded audio files. Timer feedback is generated at runtime with Android's built-in `ToneGenerator`.

Before adding images, sounds, fonts or other media in the future, their source, creator, exact license and any required attribution must be documented here.

## Software dependencies

Android and Kotlin dependencies are managed through Gradle. Their respective licenses and copyrights remain with their authors and projects.
